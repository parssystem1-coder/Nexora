**Status vocabulary, as this project uses it.** The status word is load-bearing and is chosen deliberately; R-003's own `RESOLVED` → `ACCEPTED` correction (2026-08-24) exists precisely because the wrong word misleads a future gate review.

- **OPEN** — the risk is real and unmitigated, or mitigated only partially in a way the row states explicitly.
- **PARTIALLY CLOSED** — a mitigation shipped and works for a stated set of conditions, and genuinely does not hold outside them. R-005 is the model: closed for a single running instance, open again the moment a second one runs.
- **RESOLVED** — the underlying risk is eliminated, not merely deferred or accepted. Never use this for something that is still true but tolerated.
- **ACCEPTED** — the risk is retained by an evaluated decision, permanently or until a named condition changes. The alternatives were considered on their merits and rejected, not deferred.
- **CLOSED** — fixed, with the evidence named in the row (a commit, a test that fails against the pre-fix code, or a CI run).

**Rows are append-only in spirit.** A closed or superseded row is never deleted, and superseded text is struck through in place with a dated correction above it rather than rewritten — the same no-rewrite convention this project applies to dated records. R-001, R-005 and R-008 all carry visible "superseded, not deleted" corrections; that is the intended pattern, not clutter. **Read the row before assuming a gap is unknown or already fixed** — several rows record that a plausible-sounding concern was investigated and refuted.
